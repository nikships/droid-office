Droid Office Emoji is a Modified Version of Noto Emoji. Its family name is
changed; outlines and original copyright metadata are retained. Additional
private mappings make 3770 GSUB emoji sequences available to Unity's static
glyph bake. The private mappings are not terminal input codepoints.

Run Tools/terminal-fonts.py with fonttools 4.66.1 to verify source hashes and
reproduce DroidOfficeEmoji.ttf and terminal-fonts.json. Bake the shared atlas
through TerminalFontBuilder in the live Editor. No machine fonts are needed
by the packaged app.
