# The Orchestrator

One agent for the whole building. You tell it what you want ("get CI green on every floor", "triage the open issues across my projects and put workers on the top three", "what is everyone doing?") and it plans, hires and directs workers on any floor, answers their questions, checks their work and reports back. It replaces the per-floor Team lead kiosk that was removed with the board agents (`f5023d2c`).

## What it is

- **One per office, not per floor.** The office launches and owns a single Droid session for it, in a PTY like any worker's, with the id `orchestrator` (`ORCHESTRATOR_ID` in `src/shared/orchestrator.ts`). It has no desk and is never in a floor's worker list. It starts on the first prompt, keeps its conversation across office restarts, and **New conversation** starts it over.
- **The external bot's MCP.** Its session signs in to the Office MCP as an external bot, so it gets the building-wide, owner-level surface (`office_roster`, `office_look`, `office_worker_spawn` / `prompt` / `answer` / `interrupt` / `thread`, `office_send_home`, and `team_*` across floors) instead of a desk worker's tools. The endpoint it uses is loopback-only and independent of Tailscale, so it works with `DROID_OFFICE_REMOTE_MCP=off`, in clones and in CI. Its key is its own, generated by the office and never shown in Settings → Tailscale MCP. Desk-only tools (`office_move`, `office_status`) and anything else that needs a seat are left out of its list.
- **Full owner authority.** It can hire, prompt, interrupt, answer menus and send home any worker on any floor. Worker-control actions name it in their toasts, as they name an external bot.
- **It never edits code.** It runs in the office's own `.droid-office/orchestrator/` folder, not a checkout. It reads code and plans, and every change goes to a worker it hires (`team_hire` with a structured task naming the floor's repository). Its brief says so, and the session is denied file-editing tools where Droid allows that.
- **Woken only by you and its own team.** Your prompt starts or steers it. When it is at rest and one of its subagents reports, asks, needs input or finishes, the office types a wake note into its session, as it does for a local lead. It doesn't watch the rest of the building by itself.
- **Its team.** Subagents it hires have `lead: "orchestrator"` on their own floors and wear its team markings. Turning the Orchestrator off stops its session; its team carries on and reports to nobody until it is back on.
- **Needs you.** A finished turn you haven't read, or a question or permission prompt, counts toward desktop notifications, the tab title, the Mac menu bar and Android alerts, like a worker's (`orchestratorNeedsOwner`).

## Protocol

`src/shared/orchestrator.ts` holds the types. The server sends `orchestrator` state in `welcome` (optional, so an older office simply has none) and an `{ t: 'orchestrator', state }` message to every connection, on every floor, whenever it changes. Clients send `orchestrator.prompt`, `orchestrator.interrupt`, `orchestrator.reset` and `orchestrator.set`. Its terminal, thread and menus use the ordinary worker messages (`worker.attach` / `detach`, `term.input`, `term.resize`, `thread.read`, `worker.answer`) with `workerId: "orchestrator"`, accepted from any floor.

## Settings

⚙️ Settings → **Orchestrator** (in the office and in the web, Mac and Android companions): on or off (on by default), its model and effort (office default unless chosen), and **New conversation**. Its brief is in the prompt editor under **🛰️ Orchestrator**. Settings are saved in the office's own `.droid-office/orchestrator.json`.

## Where it shows

- **3D office.** Mission Control, the glass room under the boss office, is its room on every floor: the same agent wherever you are. It stands at the head of the long table, the wall panels show what it is doing and its team across the building, and dashes run from the room to its subagents' desks on the floor. Walk up to talk to it or open its terminal. The ☰ menu, the palette and a key open it from anywhere on any floor.
- **Web and Mac companion.** A pinned Orchestrator row at the top of the sidebar on every floor, with its subagents (each tagged with its floor) beneath it. Its pane has Thread and TTY like a worker's, a composer that sends while it works and Stop, and an empty state that says what it is for.
- **Android.** A pinned Orchestrator card on Home, opening its Thread and terminal with a composer, its team across floors, its questions in Asks and notifications when it finishes or needs you.
