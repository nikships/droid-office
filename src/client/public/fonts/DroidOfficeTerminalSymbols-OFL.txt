Droid Office Terminal Symbols (DroidOfficeTerminalSymbols-Regular.woff2) is
a Modified Version of Symbols Nerd Font Mono from Nerd Fonts 3.4.0.
Copyright (c) 2016, Ryan McIntyre; icons by the Nerd Fonts contributors.
The font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (the full
license text is in OFL-LICENSE.txt in this folder).

Source: https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts/releases/tag/v3.4.0
(NerdFontsSymbolsOnly.zip). The original bundled subset covers U+E000-F8FF
and U+F0000-F0FFF. tools/terminal-symbols.py fits its advances and outlines
to Geist Mono's 0.6em cell, centers icons without stretching them, and keeps
powerline separators at both cell edges. The derivative uses a new family
name; its source copyright metadata and license remain intact.

See docs/terminal-glyphs.md for the pinned input and reproducible build.
